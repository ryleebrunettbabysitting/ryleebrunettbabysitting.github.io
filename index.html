Questions

Frequently asked

What ages do you babysit?

All ages, from infants to teens. I adjust activities and routines to fit each child.

Can we meet before booking?

Absolutely. I'm happy to do a quick meet-and-greet with your family or your dog before the first session. Just get in touch.

What do you need to know before dogsitting?

Feeding schedule, walk routine, any medications, vet contact info, and your dog's quirks.

What does dog boarding at your home look like?

Dogs stay at my house with me and have access to a dog bed, chew toys, and a spacious porch and backyard! They can also enjoy the company of my own two pups, Bella and Bridgette, both of whom are friendly.

How do I pay?

You can pay via either Venmo or cash at the end of the session.

What's your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel, no worries! Just reach out to me via text to let me know when you are able.
